# 1987 — a third-person adventure toy (implementation guide)

*2026-11-14, owner's idea; first step done: the world and the two ways to move. The program is `demos/1987.bas` (installed as `engine1999-1987`, man page `man/engine1999-1987.6`), its test `game1987test.sh` (in `make check`). Built on the mesh renderer `M3D` ([3d-mesh-subsystem.md](3d-mesh-subsystem.md)); the player's view is the third-person camera.*

## The story and what is not there yet

The year is 1987, but a different one: after an alien invasion humanity has been taken (by ships, by beams of light) and only a few people are left. John, a lean gray-haired former teacher of physics and mathematics who likes comfortable clothes, lives alone in a wooden house in the country, next to a wheat field that is ripe and has nobody to harvest it. Black, egg-shaped drones hover over the land and shoot red beams at anything suspicious. John has his bicycle and loves it.

**In this step:** the area (country roads, grass, trees, the house, the field), John and the bicycle, walking and riding. **Not in it** (ideas for the next steps, none decided): drones that react (a beam that finds John, shooting), a story and a goal, music and ambient sound (there are only footsteps, the wheel's ticking, the bell and a thud), a door you can close, jumping and the bunny hop, more buildings (a barn, other houses), the time of day, weather, items.

## The drones (added 2026-11-14, owner: "hover high above the area, scouting it")

Four black, egg-shaped drones (objects 600 .. 603; `BUILDDRONES`, `DRONESIM`, `DRONERIG`). The body is three lathe solids scaled by 2 (the egg, a gray belt ring, a red eye at the bottom; about 116 wide and 140 high) plus asymmetric armor from `DRONEARMS` (2026-11-15, owner: "a little more fearsome ... jagged edges or asymmetries"): eight jagged blades of different length round the back (varied per drone), four claws underneath (the fourth is shorter), a gun pod with a barrel and a red muzzle on one side, a broken three-piece fin on the other, two small red eyes of different size, a bent antenna with a red tip; they hover at 380 .. 485 units above the ground, bob a little and turn slowly. Each flies a route of eight way points (`DWX/DWY`; drone 0's are the `DRONEROUTE` data over the house and the wheat field, the others come from a formula): it turns towards the next point at 1.1 per second (so a curve, not a corner) and moves at 85 units a second. The beam (objects 610 .. 613, translucent, rebuilt every frame and only for drones within 2600 units of the camera) is a four-sided cone from the eye to a spot on the ground that sweeps in a circle of about 300 units under the drone, with a red disc on the ground; a dark translucent square (620 ..) is the shadow. **They only scout:** nothing checks whether a beam touches John, and they do not shoot. The look-up keys (R / F, right mouse drag up and down, right stick up and down; `CLP`) add to the camera's pitch so that you can see them; the right stick and the right-mouse drag follow the 3D settings *Invert X axis* / *Invert Y axis* (`W3K_INVX`, `W3K_INVY`, read from the program: a program may read a library's variable, only a library may not read another library's); the camera still hangs behind John.

## Sound (added 2026-11-15, owner: "drones emit a low hum ... walking and cycling noises")

`SOUNDSTART`, `AMBIENT`, `SOUNDMUTE`, `FOOTSTEP`, `FN SURF` in `demos/1987.bas`. **The ground under the feet (2026-11-21):** `FN SURF(x,y,z)` asks the engine what the floor is made of, `M3D("FLOORTEX",x,y,z)` (the picture of the highest solid triangle below the feet), so the footsteps and the tyre noise follow the pictures, also for the procedural roads, mud and rock: 304 road and 320 mud (and 320 in the wheat field: wheat) are sand (SFX 5), 305 planks, 306 tiles, 307 stone, 324 rock and 326 brick are hard (SFX 6), 308 / 321 wheat (SFX 7), everything else grass (SFX 2); the `state` line of the play-back has `sf=`, `game1987test.sh` 11i. **Drone hum:** instrument 41 `DRONEHUM` (a sine, a detuned saw an octave up (level 0.6) and a sine a twelfth up (0.45), low-passed with a cutoff of 6 times the note) held on the music channels 1 .. 4 as a never-ending note at 52, 55.3, 58.6 and 61.9 Hz (so the four hums beat against each other); every frame `VOLUME` follows the distance of that drone to the camera (`0.8 * (1 - d/2600)^2`, silent beyond 2600) and `PITCH` wobbles by 1.2 %. **Tyres:** instrument 42 `TYRE` (filtered noise) held on channel 5, `VOLUME` = speed / 250 (at most 1) times 0.1 on grass, 0.2 on a sand road, 0.26 in the wheat, 0.08 on wood, silent when the bicycle is more than 600 units from John. **Footsteps:** one effect at each heel strike of the walk cycle (the phase passes a multiple of pi), chosen by `FN SURF` (distance to the road segments < 45 units; 0 grass `SFX 2`, 1 sand `SFX 5`, 2 wood `SFX 6`: John above the terrain, 3 wheat `SFX 7`). The freewheel ticking (`SFX 3`, every 70 units rolled while not pedalling), the bell (`SFX 1`) and the crash thud (`SFX 4`) are as before. The hum is muted while paused. **Measured** (the window host with SDL's disk driver, float32 stereo): near a drone RMS about 0.2, peak 0.56 (the first try at volume 0.55 with a hotter instrument clipped: RMS 0.54; then 0.3 was too quiet for the owner to hear; the figure to tune is the volume factor in `AMBIENT`). The wood step is a single dull thump (an earlier bell-like overtone, the "pling", was removed on the owner's request). **Not heard by me:** all of it is a first guess (the pitches, the footstep sounds, the balance); the console host only checks the statements (`game1987test.sh` runs the sound procedures through `shot`).

## John: a skeleton drawn each frame

`BUILDJOHN(pose,phase,amount)` empties object 520 and builds John again every frame (about 150 mesh statements): a torso box, a head with gray hair and dark glasses, and four limbs. Each limb is two rods (`TUBE`) found by a two-bone inverse kinematics routine (`IK`: both bones equally long, the middle joint bends towards a pole vector: knees forward, elbows back), so no hand-made keyframes are needed:

- **pose 0** (on foot): the hips bob, each foot swings along a stride `amount * 9` and lifts while it moves forward, the arms swing against the legs; `amount` is the speed (0 .. 1), the phase grows with the distance walked.
- **pose 1** (riding): coordinates of the bicycle; the hips sit on the saddle, the torso leans forward, the feet follow the pedals (a circle of radius 5.5 round the bottom bracket, `CA` the crank angle), the hands hold the grips, which turn with the handlebar (`BSA` the steering angle).

## The bicycle's physics (`SIM`)

State: `BX, BY, BZ` position, `BH` heading (degrees), `BV` speed along the heading (units per second; 1 unit is about 3.2 cm, so 100 units per second is 11.5 km/h), `BS` steering input (smoothed), `BSA` the wheel's steering angle, `ST` stamina (0 .. 1).

- **Analog pedalling and sprint (2026-11-15, owner's requests; the stick changed the same day):** the bicycle has a target speed `BT` like John. **The stick's Y axis** (above the dead zone, `KFA / 0.95`) and **the left trigger** (analog, the first 8 % and the last 7 % of the pull are cut off; `KLT` 0 .. 1; it is an alternate control and works alone, on foot as well) give it the same way, linearly from `WBMIN` = 40 units a second (barely above the dead zone) to the top speed `WBMAX` = 260 (all the way); the larger wins. The forward key means plain pedalling `WPED` = 140, with SHIFT (`KSH`) the top speed. Thrust acts only below the target and stops when it is reached; a lower target (the stick eased back) is reached by coasting, the brake is A / S. A target above `WPED` is a sprint (the stronger thrust curve): it drains stamina in proportion, `0.2 * (BT - WPED) / (WBMAX - WPED)` a second. **Stamina** is one value for both (`ST`, one update at the end of `SIM`): walking above `WTIRE` = 100 units a second drains up to 0.1 a second at the top speed, otherwise 0.12 a second come back; at 0.02 John is **exhausted** (`EXH`) until it is back at 0.25: on foot the target is the slow walk `WSLOW`, on the bicycle at most `WPED`. Holding the run pull while exhausted therefore alternates slow walking and running about every 2 .. 10 seconds. Tests: 11e in `game1987test.sh`; play commands `fa v` (stick), `run v` (trigger), `shift v`.
- **Thrust** while you pedal: `78 * (1 - BV/190)`; a sprint (the trigger or SHIFT, needs stamina) `200 * (1 - BV/(1.25 * WBMAX))` and uses 0.2 stamina per second (0.12 per second come back while you do not sprint; at 0.02 the sprint is refused). Starting from a standstill takes a few seconds (the GTA feel).
- **Resistance:** `9 + 0.05 * BV` units per second squared, and **gravity along the slope**: `260 * slope` where the slope is `HT` ahead minus here over 24 units.
- **Brake** (S, DOWN, SPACE): 230 per second squared; below 0.5 it reverses slowly up to -45.
- **Steering** like a real bicycle: the wheel angle is at most 0.6 rad (34 degrees) and shrinks with speed, `0.6 / (1 + (BV/95)^2)`, and the heading changes with `BV / 36 * tan(angle)` (36 is the wheel base in units): at a standstill it cannot turn, at 30 units a second the radius is about 60, at 180 about 270.
- **Collision:** `M3D THINGMOVE` for the bicycle's thing (radius 14, the maximum); `M3D("HIT")` tells that a wall pushed it: above 120 units a second a hit is a crash (speed `-0.2 BV`, a thud, stamina -0.25), else the speed is cut to 35 %.
- **Getting on and off:** E within 70 units of the bicycle (mode 2: John slides to the saddle in 0.45 s, then mode 1); E while riding (mode 3: John steps 38 units to the left in 0.4 s, found by `THINGMOVE` so he does not end up inside a tree). The bicycle rolls on when nobody rides it.
- **The bicycle falls over (2026-11-15, owner: "A bike cannot stand upright by itself ... In the beginning, the bike should lean against the house ... If the bike is on the ground and the player picks it up, cycling should commence as before. Later we might want to get in animations for getting on/off the bike"):** the bicycle has a state `BST` (`BIKEFALL`): **1 leaning against a wall or fence** (the start: since 2026-11-15 (owner: "make the bike lean on the outside of the fence, since it's really hard to coax the bike out of the fence area") at (130, -214) on the **outside of the yard's south fence, east of the gate**, heading east, the lean 0.35 rad (20 degrees) towards the fence, the handlebar turned 50 degrees; it stays so until picked up; it used to lean against the house wall inside the yard at (100, -118), from where the bicycle had to be walked out through the gate. `game1987test.sh` 11j: John walks out of the gate and along the fence to within reach of it), **0 upright** (ridden, or riderless and rolling on: after `WFALL` = 3 seconds without a rider it falls, standing still or not), **3 falling** (the lean `BLN` grows to 90 degrees in about 0.6 s to the side `BLS`, which is chosen from the position so it varies), **4 sliding on its side** (it goes straight on in the direction it had, `BSD`, slows down by `WSLIDE` = 120 units a second squared, so a slide from 260 takes about 2 seconds and from 100 less than one, and **turns about itself** at up to 2.6 rad a second in proportion to its speed; a thud if John is within 700 units; hitting a tree or the house is as before), **5 lying** (stopped for good; the front wheel stays turned by 55 degrees), **6 being picked up** (E within 70 units: it comes back to upright in the 0.45 s of getting on, the steering angle too). E near a falling or sliding bicycle does nothing. A riderless bicycle on a slope rolls downhill (the slope term is unchanged); the freewheel ticking is silent unless it is upright. **How it is drawn:** objects have a yaw only, so the tilted bicycle is rebuilt as one object (514) by `BUILDLEAN(lean, steering)` with the same `BIKEFRAME` / `BIKEFRONT` / `BIKEREAR` / `BIKEFR` procedures as the upright one, with the global switch `XON` on: `TUBE`, `BX` (becomes six quads, `BXX`) and `SPOKES` (`SPV`) send every vertex through `PXV`, which turns the front's parts about the steering axis (`XFR`), rolls everything about the line where the wheels touch the ground and lifts it (`XLF`, so the handlebar does not sink in); built each frame while falling and being picked up (BST 3 and 6), once for 1, 4 and 5 (only the position and yaw change). Not done: getting on / off animations (the owner mentioned them for later), the bicycle as an obstacle for John, a scrape sound while sliding, leaning it against things you walk it up to. `game1987test.sh` 11g.
- **On foot (variable speed, 2026-11-15, owner's request):** John has a speed `JV` and a **target speed** `TV`; a unit is about 3.2 cm (John is 58 units tall). **Trigger / SHIFT (analog):** the trigger works alone (a forward key or stick is not needed), SHIFT needs a forward key or stick; the pull `KRA` sets the target `WSLOW + (WFAST - WSLOW) * KRA` (the larger of it and the stick's target wins); fast walking tires him (see the bicycle's analog pedalling). **Stick:** its deflection past the dead zone gives the target, linearly from `WSLOW` = 22 units a second (slow walking) to `WFAST` = 155 (running; reached at a deflection of 0.95); pushed back it gives up to -`WBACK` = -38. **Keys:** a held W / UP raises the target from `WSLOW` by `WRAMP` = 60 per second up to `WFAST` (it stops there); letting go sets it to 0; SHIFT or the left trigger set it to `WFAST` at once. John **accelerates** towards the target at `WACC` = 150 units a second squared and stops accelerating when he has reached it; towards a lower target or 0 he **slows down** at `WDEC` = 170. **Stride:** the distance of one step goes with the speed (`FN SPD1`: 0 at `WSLOW` or slower, 1 at `WFAST`) from 10 units (about 30 cm) to 30 units (about 90 cm); the walk phase `PH` advances by pi per step, the legs swing `0.6 * step` to each side. The turning rate is `150 - 0.25 * speed` degrees per second. A wall that stops him (he gets less than half of the way he meant to go) **stops him dead**: speed and the key target are 0 (owner's rule for now; the later world is to be much larger); sliding along a wall does not. **The bicycle's top speed is 30 km/h** = 8.33 m/s = 260 units a second (`WBMAX`, a hard limit also downhill; a sprint on the flat reaches it in about 4 seconds, before the stamina is gone; plain pedalling tops out near 150). Constants are set after `MAIN`; `game1987test.sh` has the checks (11d). **Not felt by me; at 155 units a second and 90 cm steps the legs make about 5 steps a second (a fast sprint): lower `WFAST` if it looks frantic.**
- **No edge of the world (since 2026-11-20):** the red tiles and the ring of border trees are gone; John and the bicycle are kept inside +-50000 (`WLIM`) only to keep the numbers small. The ground and its trees are made as John goes (next section).

## The world in chunks (roadmap item 95, step 1: a prototype, 2026-11-20)

*Owner's wish: a large, procedurally generated world (ruins of cities, hills, roads, lakes, impassable mountains, fields, meadows, forests). The plan and the reasons are in `docs/roadmap/projects.md`, item 95; this is the first step, done entirely in the BASIC program to measure what the engine would need.*

- **Chunks:** the ground is cut into squares of 900 units (9 x 9 cells of 100). Only the 7 x 7 chunks round John exist (3 in every direction; the nearest hole is more than 2700 units away, the far plane is 2400). `CHUNKS(px,py,maxn)` builds up to `maxn` of the missing ones, the **nearest first**: the game loop builds one per frame (`PLAYLOOP`), `NEWGAME` and the test command `goto` build all that are missing at once (`maxn` 64).
- **Slots:** a chunk `(cx,cy)` has the slot `(cx mod 8) + 8 (cy mod 8)`; its ground is the object `100 + slot`, its trees the objects `2000 + 24 slot + n` (`n` below 24). Building a chunk overwrites whatever the slot held (an object made again is empty: `M3D OBJECT`), nothing is allocated or freed, and the unused tree numbers are emptied (`CHCLEAR`). A 7 x 7 window never needs two chunks in one slot. `CKX / CKY` remember which chunk a slot holds.
- **The book is a library, `demos/lib/chunks.lib` (prefix `CK_`, 2026-11-20):** it knows which chunks exist and builds nothing (a library cannot call the program's procedures), so the program **pulls**: `CK_SETUP(900,3,1,3)` once (size, radius, fine, medium), `CK_UPDATE(px,py)` every frame, then `IF FN CK_NEXT() THEN` build the chunk named by `FN CK_CX()`, `CK_CY()`, `CK_CSLOT()`, `CK_CLEVEL()`, `CK_CNEW()` and call `CK_DONE` (the program's `CHUNKS(px,py,maxn)` is that loop, one pass a frame, 64 = all at once). Also `FN CK_SLOT`, `CK_HAS`, `CK_LEVELAT`, `CK_STALE`, `CK_FORGET`; the header of the library file lists them. `chunkstest.sh` tests it.
- **Levels of detail (2026-11-20):** the chunk's level comes from its ring distance from John's chunk (the larger of the two differences): up to 1 chunk away **level 0** (9 x 9 cells of 100 units, 9 chunks), farther away **level 1** (3 x 3 cells of 300 units: 18 triangles instead of 162). When John moves, the chunks that cross a ring are built again at the new level (`CK_CNEW()` is 0 then: only the ground, the trees stay); every grid has a **skirt** of 24 units under its edges (`M3D TERRAIN`'s `skirt`) so that no crack opens between a fine and a coarse neighbour. Building 148 chunks (the test's jumps) takes 6 ticks of 1/60 s (13 with every chunk fine).
- **A chunk is a pure function of `(cx,cy)`:** built again later it comes out exactly the same (the test compares two runs). The ground height is the one function `HT(x,y)` of the position, so the borders of chunks match. Randomness comes from `HASH(x,y,salt)`, the engine's hash of up to four integers (0 .. 1, 53 bits, pure and the same on every machine, `docs/noise.md`; the first prototype had its own in BASIC, `HSH`, until 2026-11-20), never from `RND`, so building a chunk does not disturb the game's random numbers. A tree's yaw comes from the hash too (`TYW`, -1 = random as before).
- **The hand-made area** is the chunks -4 .. 3 (the old world, x and y from -3600 to 3600): the house, the yard, the roads, the groves, the field and the scarecrow stay as they were. Outside it the rules below apply.
- **Kinds of ground:** the world is cut into regions of 1800 units; each region has one **feature point** at a hashed place with a kind: forest 38 %, meadow 28 %, field 20 %, plain 14 % (`CHREGIONS`). A cell takes the kind of the **nearest feature point** among the 3 x 3 regions round the chunk (`GKIND`), which makes organic borders (staircase shaped, because a cell is one picture). Forest: dry grass (picture 302) and trees; meadow: lush grass (303); field: soil (320); plain: the old grass noise (`CELLTEX`). `OCELL` picks the picture.
- **Forest:** `CHTREES` tries 60 hashed places in the chunk; a place in forest is taken with 70 %, at least 70 units from the other trees of the chunk, up to 24 trees; kind (broadleaf 45 %, pine 35 %, poplar 20 %), size and yaw come from the hash.
- **Measured** (console host, the mesh buffers of the window not included): a chunk with trees takes on average 0.4 ticks (about 6 ms), at most 1 tick (1/60 s) of the BASIC program; 49 chunks at once about 0.3 s. Seen under Xvfb at several far places (fields with furrows, forest floors with trees, meadows). **Not measured:** the cost in the window host on a real GPU (new objects mean new buffers), walking at the bicycle's speed across many chunks.
- **Test:** `game1987test.sh` part 11f / 11f2 (the `chunks` command prints how many of the 49 chunks are present, how many are fine and how many stale (missing or of the wrong level), the trees in them and a signature): walking east past the old edge at 3420, two far jumps (20000 / -15000 and -9000 / 7000), the same world in two runs.
- **The ground in C (2026-11-20, next step of item 95):** `HT` asks the engine (`M3D("GROUND",x,y)`; the function is `base + amp * FBM(...)` times the flat boxes of the house and the wheat field, `M3D GROUND` / `M3D GROUNDFLAT`, see `docs/3d-mesh-subsystem.md`), and a chunk's ground is one `M3D TERRAIN` call with the cells' pictures as a string, so the terrain is no longer periodic and the same everywhere (the hills are not the old sines: the world looks a little different), and building 130 chunks takes 20 ticks of 1/60 s instead of 40 (the rest is the pictures' string and the trees).
- **The wild world (2026-11-20, the rest of item 95):** outside the hand-made area (kept free by `M3D GROUNDQUIET`, 3900 units round the house, fading in over 1200) the ground function (`SETGROUND`) has **mountains** (`M3D MOUNTAINS 1987,500,260,5,0.05,0.4`: ridged noise up to 260 high, about 8 % of the plane above 120) and **lakes** (`M3D LAKES 4711,900,44,0.35,0.55`: basins 44 deep, about one chunk in six has water). `FN BLOCKED(x,y)`: ground above 120 (`MTLIM`), steeper than 22 units of height over 20 (`SLLIM`, about 48 degrees) or lower than 12 under the water level `WZ` -12 stops John and the bicycle dead (the position is put back, speed 0, like a wall); wading is possible down to -24. Cell pictures (`OCELL`): rock (324: gray blotches) on ground above 70 or steeper than 45 over 100 units, mud (320) under water, else the kind of ground; trees keep to ground between `WZ + 2` and 70. **Water:** a chunk whose ground dips below `WZ` (5 x 5 samples) gets a translucent plane at `WZ` (`CHWATER`, object 4000 + slot, picture 325). **City ruins** (`CHRUINS` / `CHCITY`, objects 5000 + 32 slot + n): big regions of 7200 units have a site with a chance of 30 % (a hashed place, radius 450 .. 900); inside it the ground is cut into lots of 180 units, 70 % hold a ruin of up to four brick walls (picture 326, each there with 78 %, 12 .. 172 high, taller towards the middle) and three heaps of rubble; lots on water, steep or high ground stay empty. **Roads** (`CHROADS`, object 8000 + slot): the region points are joined to their east and south neighbours with a chance of 45 %, a wandering path (noise) of 50 unit steps that meets the points exactly, a strip 110 wide 3 above the ground (picture 304), not over water, steep or high ground. **Hay bales** in the fields (`CHBALES`, instances of the bale 343, up to 6 a chunk, objects 3600 + 6 slot + n). The test (`game1987test.sh` 11f3 / 11f4): walking east from (11200, 0) John stops at the foot of a mountain, from (-14400, -5900) at a lake's shore, the window round (1350, 8550) has ruins, roads and bales.
- **Not done in the wild world:** roads have no bridges (they stop at water); trees stand on roads; nothing to find in the ruins; the drones' routes ignore the new land; the coarse level (300 unit cells) can hide a road or a ruin's foot under the ground in the distance; the hay bales are plain boxes; the meadows have no extra content.
- **Known limits of this step** (the plan has the rest): no lakes, mountains, roads, or ruins; kinds of ground are only pictures and trees (no crops in the fields, no flowers in the meadows); the Voronoi test looks at 3 x 3 regions only (a rare border chunk can differ from its neighbour's idea); the drones' routes and the sound ambient know nothing about chunks; the wheat field's old objects are the only crop.

## The camera: an agent (`CAMAGENT`, since 2026-11-15)

The owner's idea (2026-11-15): **the camera is a creature of its own, an NPC**, instead of a formula that is tweaked until it does not jump. `CAMUPDATE` only calls `CAMAGENT`, which keeps the agent's state: a **position** `CMX, CMY, CMZ` (the eye; the feet given to `M3D CAMERA` are the eye minus 46), a **velocity** `CMVX .. CMVZ`, a **view** `CMYW` (yaw), `CMPT` (pitch) with the angular velocities `CMWY`, `CMWP`, and a **behavior** `CMB`. It moves like a point mass chasing the place it wants to be at: acceleration = 26 * (error) + 10.2 * (the velocity of the wished place - its own velocity) (a spring and a damper, critically damped; the velocity of the wished place is measured, so a camera behind the bicycle does not lag), limited to 1800 units a second squared and 900 a second; the view is the same kind of controller (90 / 19, at most 540 degrees a second turning, 360 pitching; a lazier 28 / 10.6, a glance, when it turns to a point of interest). Nothing is set to a value from one frame to the next, so nothing can jump: the smoothing the camera had before (`CZS` for John's height, `ZOS` for the height of the focus, `DWS`, `DSS`) is gone except the first two.

**Behaviors (`CMB`):** **0 follow**: the place wanted is behind John (as seen from where the camera looks: the azimuth `CYW`, which follows his heading with a delay, 3.4 / 2.4 a second) and above him (12 degrees), 125 units away (155 + 0.18 * speed on the bicycle), looking at his head. **1 directed**: the player uses the camera controls (Z / X, the right stick's x, the right mouse button and dragging turn it round John, PAGE UP / PAGE DOWN raise and lower it, R / F and the right stick's y and dragging up and down tilt the view): `READKEYS` only turns them into **instructions** (`CMIN` says that one is given; `CO` the wished azimuth offset, `CP` the elevation, `CLP` the look pitch; they are not applied to the camera but to the place it wants), and the camera carries them out with its own acceleration: orbiting is a circling course that it has to accelerate into and brake out of. When the instruction stops it waits a second (`CMHOLD`) and then resumes behavior 0: the offsets die away (`CO` and `CMAZ` at 1.1 a second) and the camera flies back behind John. **2 circling** and **3 watching**: when the player has done nothing at all (no camera control, not walking, riding or using anything: `CMIDLE`) for `CMNEXT` seconds (7 at first, later 6 .. 12 after an action) the camera acts on its own (`CAMSTART`): either it **circles John** for 8 .. 12 seconds (an azimuth that grows by 14 .. 24 degrees a second, 9 degrees higher) or it **watches a point of interest** for 4 .. 6.5 seconds, the next useful one in the cycle bicycle (when John is on foot and within 900 units), nearest drone (within 1800), the house, the scarecrow, the gate (`CMPC`, `CMPK` 0 point / 1 drone); after a point of interest it prefers a circle (60 %). **Any input of the player ends 2 and 3 at once** and the camera goes back behind John. **Walls:** as before, three rays from John's head to the wished place (straight and 9 degrees to each side) shorten the distance, and since the agent has a body, one more ray from John's head to the camera itself pulls it in front of anything it would be behind (its velocity is cut to 30 %); it stays 10 units above the floor.

**The indicators (`CAMHUD`):** the player must see that the camera acts on its own: a **red dot that blinks, with REC**, in the upper left corner, and in the lower right corner a row of **seven red glyphs of an alien script** (a glyph is three to five strokes between points of a 7 x 10 grid, `NEWGLYPH`; drawn twice, shifted by one pixel, as a shadow) one of which is made new every 0.25 .. 1.85 seconds (every 0.08 .. 0.58 when the camera is busy with behavior 2 or 3). **They are shown in all behaviors but 1** (when the player directs the camera, it does what it is told: the indicators go). The text line at the top (messages, "E: GET ON THE BICYCLE") moved from y = 6 to y = 22 for the dot. **Tests (`game1987test.sh` 11k, and the play commands `orbit v` and `look`...):** the camera follows from behind (yaw within 10 degrees of John's heading), `orbit 1` for 1.5 s takes it more than 60 degrees round, it is back behind him 5 s after the instruction stops, left alone it is in behavior 2 or 3 10 s after the last input, and a key ends it. **Not seen in motion by me; the numbers (acceleration limits, the 7 s, the durations, the cycle of points of interest) are a first guess.** Ideas not done: the camera noticing things on its own (a drone's beam coming near, the bicycle crashing), a wobble of the REC glyphs when the camera is moving fast, the camera zooming in when watching a point of interest, a second point of view (a drone's), the camera refusing an instruction (the alien rule)...

## The test mode and `game1987test.sh`

`G1987_HEADLESS=1` runs the program in the console host without any picture (no graphics setup, no banner, no sounds), `G1987_PLAY=file` plays a file of commands one per line: `f v` and `t v` (forward / pedal and turn, -1 .. 1), `fa v` (analog forward, -1 .. 1), `run v` (the run / sprint pull 0 .. 1, analog), `brake v`, `use`, `wait seconds` (fixed steps of 1/60 s, so every run is the same), `goto x y heading` (John), `bike x y heading`, `state` and `cam` (print `key=value` lines), `ray x y z yaw pitch`, `zoom d`, `co deg`, `cp deg` (camera), and in the window host `shot name` (a screenshot file) and `bench n` (frames per second; appends to `bench.txt`). The test makes about 25 checks: the walk through the open door and against the wall, mounting, no turning on the spot, pedalling up to speed, the sprint and the stamina, coasting, braking, dismounting, a crash into the fence, turning at speed, the camera, rays and that two runs give the same numbers.

Under Xvfb (no GPU): world build 0.6 s in the console host; software renderer 16 .. 18 ms a frame at 320 x 132 (7000 .. 8600 triangles drawn), Mesa's CPU OpenGL 33 ms (not meaningful: the owner's GPU is the case to measure with `bench`). ## Facts found while writing it

- A user `FUNC` needs `FN` also inside arguments and indices: `RN(5)` without `FN` is an array subscript.
- A picture number with no picture draws the palette color of that number: keep pictures at 301 and up (a green stripe across John's face was picture 17, the leaves, used as "palette color 17").
- `M3D THINGMOVE` collides with *every* solid object including props that the camera ray ignores (a mailbox 14 units from the bicycle's start stopped it from rolling: the test found that).
- The headless run must skip the `GFX` setup (`W3K_SETUP`, textures, `W3K_VIEW`, `M3D COLORMAP`, `SFX` definitions): they are "not available in the console host".
