# 1987 — a third-person adventure toy (implementation guide)

*2026-11-14, owner's idea; first step done: the world and the two ways to move. The program is `demos/1987.bas` (installed as `engine1999-1987`, man page `man/engine1999-1987.6`), its test `game1987test.sh` (in `make check`). Built on the mesh renderer `M3D` ([3d-mesh-subsystem.md](3d-mesh-subsystem.md)); the player's view is the third-person camera.*

## The story and what is not there yet

The year is 1987, but a different one: after an alien invasion humanity has been taken (by ships, by beams of light) and only a few people are left. John, a lean gray-haired former teacher of physics and mathematics who likes comfortable clothes, lives alone in a wooden house in the country, next to a wheat field that is ripe and has nobody to harvest it. Black, egg-shaped drones hover over the land and shoot red beams at anything suspicious. John has his bicycle and loves it.

**In this step:** the area (country roads, grass, trees, the house, the field), John and the bicycle, walking and riding. **Not in it** (ideas for the next steps, none decided): drones that react (a beam that finds John, shooting), a story and a goal, music and ambient sound (there are only footsteps, the wheel's ticking, the bell and a thud), a door you can close, jumping and the bunny hop, more buildings (a barn, other houses), the time of day, weather, items.

## The drones (added 2026-11-14, owner: "hover high above the area, scouting it")

Four black, egg-shaped drones (objects 600 .. 603; `BUILDDRONES`, `DRONESIM`, `DRONERIG`). The body is three lathe solids scaled by 2 (the egg, a gray belt ring, a red eye at the bottom; about 116 wide and 140 high) plus asymmetric armor from `DRONEARMS` (2026-11-15, owner: "a little more fearsome ... jagged edges or asymmetries"): eight jagged blades of different length round the back (varied per drone), four claws underneath (the fourth is shorter), a gun pod with a barrel and a red muzzle on one side, a broken three-piece fin on the other, two small red eyes of different size, a bent antenna with a red tip; they hover at 380 .. 485 units above the ground, bob a little and turn slowly. Each flies a route of eight way points (`DWX/DWY`; drone 0's are the `DRONEROUTE` data over the house and the wheat field, the others come from a formula): it turns towards the next point at 1.1 per second (so a curve, not a corner) and moves at 85 units a second. The beam (objects 610 .. 613, translucent, rebuilt every frame and only for drones within 2600 units of the camera) is a four-sided cone from the eye to a spot on the ground that sweeps in a circle of about 300 units under the drone, with a red disc on the ground; a dark translucent square (620 ..) is the shadow. **They only scout:** nothing checks whether a beam touches John, and they do not shoot. The look-up keys (R / F, right mouse drag up and down, right stick up and down; `CLP`) add to the camera's pitch so that you can see them; the right stick and the right-mouse drag follow the 3D settings *Invert X axis* / *Invert Y axis* (`W3K_INVX`, `W3K_INVY`, read from the program: a program may read a library's variable, only a library may not read another library's); the camera still hangs behind John.

## Sound (added 2026-11-15, owner: "drones emit a low hum ... walking and cycling noises")

`SOUNDSTART`, `AMBIENT`, `SOUNDMUTE`, `FOOTSTEP`, `FN SURF` in `demos/1987.bas`. **Drone hum:** instrument 41 `DRONEHUM` (a sine, a detuned saw an octave up (level 0.6) and a sine a twelfth up (0.45), low-passed with a cutoff of 6 times the note) held on the music channels 1 .. 4 as a never-ending note at 52, 55.3, 58.6 and 61.9 Hz (so the four hums beat against each other); every frame `VOLUME` follows the distance of that drone to the camera (`0.8 * (1 - d/2600)^2`, silent beyond 2600) and `PITCH` wobbles by 1.2 %. **Tyres:** instrument 42 `TYRE` (filtered noise) held on channel 5, `VOLUME` = speed / 250 (at most 1) times 0.1 on grass, 0.2 on a sand road, 0.26 in the wheat, 0.08 on wood, silent when the bicycle is more than 600 units from John. **Footsteps:** one effect at each heel strike of the walk cycle (the phase passes a multiple of pi), chosen by `FN SURF` (distance to the road segments < 45 units; 0 grass `SFX 2`, 1 sand `SFX 5`, 2 wood `SFX 6`: John above the terrain, 3 wheat `SFX 7`). The freewheel ticking (`SFX 3`, every 70 units rolled while not pedalling), the bell (`SFX 1`) and the crash thud (`SFX 4`) are as before. The hum is muted while paused. **Measured** (the window host with SDL's disk driver, float32 stereo): near a drone RMS about 0.2, peak 0.56 (the first try at volume 0.55 with a hotter instrument clipped: RMS 0.54; then 0.3 was too quiet for the owner to hear; the figure to tune is the volume factor in `AMBIENT`). The wood step is a single dull thump (an earlier bell-like overtone, the "pling", was removed on the owner's request). **Not heard by me:** all of it is a first guess (the pitches, the footstep sounds, the balance); the console host only checks the statements (`game1987test.sh` runs the sound procedures through `shot`).

## John: a skeleton drawn each frame

`BUILDJOHN(pose,phase,amount)` empties object 520 and builds John again every frame (about 150 mesh statements): a torso box, a head with gray hair and dark glasses, and four limbs. Each limb is two rods (`TUBE`) found by a two-bone inverse kinematics routine (`IK`: both bones equally long, the middle joint bends towards a pole vector: knees forward, elbows back), so no hand-made keyframes are needed:

- **pose 0** (on foot): the hips bob, each foot swings along a stride `amount * 9` and lifts while it moves forward, the arms swing against the legs; `amount` is the speed (0 .. 1), the phase grows with the distance walked.
- **pose 1** (riding): coordinates of the bicycle; the hips sit on the saddle, the torso leans forward, the feet follow the pedals (a circle of radius 5.5 round the bottom bracket, `CA` the crank angle), the hands hold the grips, which turn with the handlebar (`BSA` the steering angle).
- **The figure's look (2026-11-15, owner: "make the human figure look more realistic: A round head like an upside down egg shape, round, perhaps jagged hair, a face (what is the black bar supposed to be? eyes or moustache?) with eyes, mouth and nose. The pelvis should align in length and width with the lower part of the torso"):** the black bar of the first version was meant as a pair of eyes (a dark strip across the face); it is gone. `HEAD(O,CX,CY,CZ)` builds the head from the chin up: an egg standing on its narrow end (profile in `LRZ` / `LRR`: 12 sides, 10.5 high, 8.6 wide, widest at 55 % of its height, the chin radius 0.5, `FN HPRF` interpolates the profile), a **cap of jagged gray hair** (five rings pushed out 0.55 from the skull, the lowest at the forehead in front, over the ears at the sides, at the nape at the back, ragged in height and radius, two shades alternate), a neck, two ears, **eyes** (white with a dark blue pupil), gray eyebrows, a **nose** and a **mouth** (dark red); the head faces +x in both poses. `FRUST` builds a box that is smaller at one end from the globals `F1X, F1Y, F2X, F2Y, FC1X, FC1Y, FC2X, FC2Y` (half sizes and centres of the bottom and the top): the **torso** narrows to a 7.2 x 12 waist and widens to 8.8 x 14.8 at the chest, the **pelvis** has exactly the torso's bottom as its top (7.2 x 12, flaring to 7.8 x 13 at the thighs) and a **brown belt** (`BELT`) covers the seam; the hips are 3.6 apart (the legs' outer edge matches the pelvis). Riding: the pelvis block is 8.4 x 13.6 to match the two torso tubes. Seen in Xvfb screenshots from the front, the side, the back and riding; not seen: from below, in motion close up. Ideas not done: glasses (he is a former teacher), a collar and sleeves, hands with fingers, a head that turns to look, a pair of shoes with soles and laces, a breathing motion.

The bicycle's wheels are a 12-sided ring of rods (built once) plus three spoke ribbons that are rebuilt with the wheel angle `WA`, which is what makes them look like they spin. Everything is two sided (flag 16) and passable / see-through (12) so the camera ray and the physics never see them.

## The bicycle's physics (`SIM`)

State: `BX, BY, BZ` position, `BH` heading (degrees), `BV` speed along the heading (units per second; 1 unit is about 3.2 cm, so 100 units per second is 11.5 km/h), `BS` steering input (smoothed), `BSA` the wheel's steering angle, `ST` stamina (0 .. 1).

- **Analog pedalling and sprint (2026-11-15, owner's requests; the stick changed the same day):** the bicycle has a target speed `BT` like John. **The stick's Y axis** (above the dead zone, `KFA / 0.95`) and **the left trigger** (analog, the first 8 % and the last 7 % of the pull are cut off; `KLT` 0 .. 1; it is an alternate control and works alone, on foot as well) give it the same way, linearly from `WBMIN` = 40 units a second (barely above the dead zone) to the top speed `WBMAX` = 260 (all the way); the larger wins. The forward key means plain pedalling `WPED` = 140, with SHIFT (`KSH`) the top speed. Thrust acts only below the target and stops when it is reached; a lower target (the stick eased back) is reached by coasting, the brake is A / S. A target above `WPED` is a sprint (the stronger thrust curve): it drains stamina in proportion, `0.2 * (BT - WPED) / (WBMAX - WPED)` a second. **Stamina** is one value for both (`ST`, one update at the end of `SIM`): walking above `WTIRE` = 100 units a second drains up to 0.1 a second at the top speed, otherwise 0.12 a second come back; at 0.02 John is **exhausted** (`EXH`) until it is back at 0.25: on foot the target is the slow walk `WSLOW`, on the bicycle at most `WPED`. Holding the run pull while exhausted therefore alternates slow walking and running about every 2 .. 10 seconds. Tests: 11e in `game1987test.sh`; play commands `fa v` (stick), `run v` (trigger), `shift v`.
- **Thrust** while you pedal: `78 * (1 - BV/190)`; a sprint (the trigger or SHIFT, needs stamina) `200 * (1 - BV/(1.25 * WBMAX))` and uses 0.2 stamina per second (0.12 per second come back while you do not sprint; at 0.02 the sprint is refused). Starting from a standstill takes a few seconds (the GTA feel).
- **Resistance:** `9 + 0.05 * BV` units per second squared, and **gravity along the slope**: `260 * slope` where the slope is `HT` ahead minus here over 24 units.
- **Brake** (S, DOWN, SPACE): 230 per second squared; below 0.5 it reverses slowly up to -45.
- **Steering** like a real bicycle: the wheel angle is at most 0.6 rad (34 degrees) and shrinks with speed, `0.6 / (1 + (BV/95)^2)`, and the heading changes with `BV / 36 * tan(angle)` (36 is the wheel base in units): at a standstill it cannot turn, at 30 units a second the radius is about 60, at 180 about 270.
- **Collision:** `M3D THINGMOVE` for the bicycle's thing (radius 14, the maximum); `M3D("HIT")` tells that a wall pushed it: above 120 units a second a hit is a crash (speed `-0.2 BV`, a thud, stamina -0.25), else the speed is cut to 35 %.
- **Getting on and off:** E within 70 units of the bicycle (mode 2: John slides to the saddle in 0.45 s, then mode 1); E while riding (mode 3: John steps 38 units to the left in 0.4 s, found by `THINGMOVE` so he does not end up inside a tree). The bicycle rolls on when nobody rides it.
- **The bicycle falls over (2026-11-15, owner: "A bike cannot stand upright by itself ... In the beginning, the bike should lean against the house ... If the bike is on the ground and the player picks it up, cycling should commence as before. Later we might want to get in animations for getting on/off the bike"):** the bicycle has a state `BST` (`BIKEFALL`): **1 leaning against the house** (the start: at (100, -118) east of the porch, inside the yard, heading east, the lean 0.35 rad (20 degrees) towards the wall, the handlebar turned 50 degrees; it stays so until picked up), **0 upright** (ridden, or riderless and rolling on: after `WFALL` = 3 seconds without a rider it falls, standing still or not), **3 falling** (the lean `BLN` grows to 90 degrees in about 0.6 s to the side `BLS`, which is chosen from the position so it varies), **4 sliding on its side** (it goes straight on in the direction it had, `BSD`, slows down by `WSLIDE` = 120 units a second squared, so a slide from 260 takes about 2 seconds and from 100 less than one, and **turns about itself** at up to 2.6 rad a second in proportion to its speed; a thud if John is within 700 units; hitting a tree or the house is as before), **5 lying** (stopped for good; the front wheel stays turned by 55 degrees), **6 being picked up** (E within 70 units: it comes back to upright in the 0.45 s of getting on, the steering angle too). E near a falling or sliding bicycle does nothing. A riderless bicycle on a slope rolls downhill (the slope term is unchanged); the freewheel ticking is silent unless it is upright. **How it is drawn:** objects have a yaw only, so the tilted bicycle is rebuilt as one object (514) by `BUILDLEAN(lean, steering)` with the same `BIKEFRAME` / `BIKEFRONT` / `BIKEREAR` / `BIKEFR` procedures as the upright one, with the global switch `XON` on: `TUBE`, `BX` (becomes six quads, `BXX`) and `SPOKES` (`SPV`) send every vertex through `PXV`, which turns the front's parts about the steering axis (`XFR`), rolls everything about the line where the wheels touch the ground and lifts it (`XLF`, so the handlebar does not sink in); built each frame while falling and being picked up (BST 3 and 6), once for 1, 4 and 5 (only the position and yaw change). Not done: getting on / off animations (the owner mentioned them for later), the bicycle as an obstacle for John, a scrape sound while sliding, leaning it against things you walk it up to. `game1987test.sh` 11g.
- **On foot (variable speed, 2026-11-15, owner's request):** John has a speed `JV` and a **target speed** `TV`; a unit is about 3.2 cm (John is 58 units tall). **Trigger / SHIFT (analog):** the trigger works alone (a forward key or stick is not needed), SHIFT needs a forward key or stick; the pull `KRA` sets the target `WSLOW + (WFAST - WSLOW) * KRA` (the larger of it and the stick's target wins); fast walking tires him (see the bicycle's analog pedalling). **Stick:** its deflection past the dead zone gives the target, linearly from `WSLOW` = 22 units a second (slow walking) to `WFAST` = 155 (running; reached at a deflection of 0.95); pushed back it gives up to -`WBACK` = -38. **Keys:** a held W / UP raises the target from `WSLOW` by `WRAMP` = 60 per second up to `WFAST` (it stops there); letting go sets it to 0; SHIFT or the left trigger set it to `WFAST` at once. John **accelerates** towards the target at `WACC` = 150 units a second squared and stops accelerating when he has reached it; towards a lower target or 0 he **slows down** at `WDEC` = 170. **Stride:** the distance of one step goes with the speed (`FN SPD1`: 0 at `WSLOW` or slower, 1 at `WFAST`) from 10 units (about 30 cm) to 30 units (about 90 cm); the walk phase `PH` advances by pi per step, the legs swing `0.6 * step` to each side. The turning rate is `150 - 0.25 * speed` degrees per second. A wall that stops him (he gets less than half of the way he meant to go) **stops him dead**: speed and the key target are 0 (owner's rule for now; the later world is to be much larger); sliding along a wall does not. **The bicycle's top speed is 30 km/h** = 8.33 m/s = 260 units a second (`WBMAX`, a hard limit also downhill; a sprint on the flat reaches it in about 4 seconds, before the stamina is gone; plain pedalling tops out near 150). Constants are set after `MAIN`; `game1987test.sh` has the checks (11d). **Not felt by me; at 155 units a second and 90 cm steps the legs make about 5 steps a second (a fast sprint): lower `WFAST` if it looks frantic.**
- **The edge of the world** is the only limit that is not an object you can see: John and the bicycle are put back at +-3420 (`WLIM`) and stop dead (speed 0, key target 0). It is marked by a band of alternating bright and dark red tiles on the ground just outside the line (`BUILDEDGE`, objects 330 .. 333, passable, 100 x 30 units each, 2.5 above the ground); the ring of trees stands behind it. **There are no other invisible walls (owner's rule, 2026-11-15):** what stops John is a tree, the house, the yard's fence and its props, or the edge. Until then the barrel at the yard was built with `LATHE(322,4,color,301)`, where the last argument is the scale: a barrel 301 times its size, a ring wall 2700 .. 3300 units round the yard that was solid but could not be seen (stops at (-2564, 644) and many others on open ground); it is a barrel of 1 now. `game1987test.sh` 11f; a sweep of 1764 walks (11 x 11 start points, four headings, 2.5 s at a slow walk) leaves only stops at trunks, fences and the house. **Known quirk, not chased:** walking exactly along x = 0 (a seam between two terrain chunks, and John's start line) a collision at the cell corners y = -1200 and -1300 stops him (the same walk at x = 1, 37, 50, 100 is free); the cause is in the collision of `M3D THINGMOVE` and only shows with an exactly aligned heading.

## The camera (`CAMUPDATE`)

The player of `M3D` is the camera: it is placed every frame with `M3D CAMERA` (the feet are the eye minus 46). Wanted: behind John at 125 units (155 + 0.18 * speed on the bicycle), 12 degrees above him, looking at his chest. The heading it follows lags behind John's (rate 3.4 / 2.4 per second), `CO` and `CP` are the user's turn and tilt (they swing back by themselves). **Occlusion:** the camera is first put at John's head looking backwards to the wished place and `M3D("SHOOT")` measures the distance to the first object (the objects that must not block it, the props and trees and the whole mesh of John and the bicycle, carry flag 8 *see-through* and are skipped); the camera moves in to 12 units before it (at least 20). It is also kept 10 units above the floor (`M3D("FLOORAT")` and `HT`).

## The test mode and `game1987test.sh`

`G1987_HEADLESS=1` runs the program in the console host without any picture (no graphics setup, no banner, no sounds), `G1987_PLAY=file` plays a file of commands one per line: `f v` and `t v` (forward / pedal and turn, -1 .. 1), `fa v` (analog forward, -1 .. 1), `run v` (the run / sprint pull 0 .. 1, analog), `brake v`, `use`, `wait seconds` (fixed steps of 1/60 s, so every run is the same), `goto x y heading` (John), `bike x y heading`, `state` and `cam` (print `key=value` lines), `ray x y z yaw pitch`, `zoom d`, `co deg`, `cp deg` (camera), and in the window host `shot name` (a screenshot file) and `bench n` (frames per second; appends to `bench.txt`). The test makes about 25 checks: the walk through the open door and against the wall, mounting, no turning on the spot, pedalling up to speed, the sprint and the stamina, coasting, braking, dismounting, a crash into the fence, turning at speed, the camera, rays and that two runs give the same numbers.

## Measured

Under Xvfb (no GPU): world build 0.6 s in the console host; software renderer 16 .. 18 ms a frame at 320 x 132 (7000 .. 8600 triangles drawn), Mesa's CPU OpenGL 33 ms (not meaningful: the owner's GPU is the case to measure with `bench`). The default render size follows the 3D settings (640 x 264 with OpenGL, else 320 x 132).

## Facts found while writing it

- A user `FUNC` needs `FN` also inside arguments and indices: `RN(5)` without `FN` is an array subscript.
- A picture number with no picture draws the palette color of that number: keep pictures at 301 and up (a green stripe across John's face was picture 17, the leaves, used as "palette color 17").
- `M3D THINGMOVE` collides with *every* solid object including props that the camera ray ignores (a mailbox 14 units from the bicycle's start stopped it from rolling: the test found that).
- The headless run must skip the `GFX` setup (`W3K_SETUP`, textures, `W3K_VIEW`, `M3D COLORMAP`, `SFX` definitions): they are "not available in the console host".
